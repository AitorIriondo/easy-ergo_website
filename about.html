About Us

About Us

We Are Ergonomists and Researchers In Virtual Ergonomics

At EasyErgo, we blend the power of innovative technology with a deep passion for occupational health. Our journey brings together years of experience in scientific research, software development, physiotherapy, and workplace ergonomics — a combination that allows us to see workplace challenges from every angle. We know the realities of working life, the science behind human well-being, and the potential of digital tools to bridge the gap between the two.

This unique mix of skills and perspectives drives us to design solutions that are both powerful and practical. Our tools are built to be intuitive for professionals in the field, yet grounded in rigorous, evidence-based methods. We believe that improving occupational health is not just about reducing risks, but also about enabling people to work at their best — safer, healthier, and with greater satisfaction.

By combining our expertise, we’ve created EasyErgo as a platform where meaningful technology meets real-world impact, helping professionals everywhere make confident, data-driven decisions that truly improve working lives.

Our Publications

EasyErgo is the result of years of rigorous research in digital human modeling, ergonomics, and AI-driven optimization. Our work is grounded in scientific methods and validated through peer-reviewed publications. We are committed to bridging the gap between academia and industry by transforming advanced methodologies into user-friendly tools that enhance productivity and promote worker well-being.
