About

Researchers building the ergonomics tool we wished we had.

Who we are

A small team. A long research lineage.

EasyErgo combines deep occupational-health research with modern computer vision and 3D reconstruction. We come from physiotherapy, software engineering and academic research in digital human modelling — and we built the platform because the everyday ergonomics workflow is still mostly stopwatches and paper forms.

The goal is simple to state and hard to do well: every workstation, every shift, scored the same way, with the source and version on every result so an audit always has a clear trail. Same workflow whether you're an ergonomist with a thousand assessments behind you or a plant manager running your first one.

We're based in Skövde, Sweden — spun out of research at the University of Skövde, the Swedish hub for digital manufacturing research — and we work closely with the academic community there.
